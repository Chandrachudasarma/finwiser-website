Finwiser app: screens for accessibility audit

This page lists every screen in the Finwiser mobile app, with screenshots and our accessibility notes for each. It is for firms quoting for, or carrying out, our audit under SEBI's digital-accessibility circular.

  • AppFinwiser, iOS and Android
  • Built withFlutter, one code base for both platforms
  • Minimum OSiOS 16 · Android 9 (API 28)
  • Scope11 journeys, 38 screen states
  • StandardsWCAG 2.1 AA · GIGW 3.0 · IS 17802
  • CircularSEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/111

About this audit

What we ask of the auditor

  • Test every journey below on a real iPhone with VoiceOver and a real Android phone with TalkBack.
  • Test at the largest system text size, with reduce-motion on, and with a switch or external keyboard where the platform supports it.
  • Include usability testing with persons with disabilities, as §5.1 of the circular asks.
  • Deliver the report in the format of Tables C1–C4 (circular 2025/131), signed by an IAAP-certified professional with the certificate number.
  • Recheck after we fix what you find, and confirm the closure.

What we have already done

  • An internal review in November 2026: automated checks on every screen, code review, and a check against SEBI §4 (help for persons with disabilities during sign-up).
  • Each automated check now runs as a test on every change: tap targets (48 dp Android, 44 pt iOS), labelled controls, text contrast, and layout at 200% text.
  • We fixed what the review found. The test build you receive includes those fixes.

Access

  • We share a test build through TestFlight (iOS) and Play testing (Android), with a test account that holds sample financial data.
  • Signing up uses a one-time code sent by SMS. The test account avoids the need for a real bank account.

Outside our app

  • Account Aggregator consent page. To link bank accounts, the user approves a consent on the Account Aggregator's own page, which is operated by an RBI-licensed Account Aggregator, not by us. Please note any problems you find there separately.
  • System screens such as Face ID or fingerprint prompts, the phone dialer, and the email app.

Across the whole app

App-wide behaviour, so it is not repeated on every screen.
AreaWhat the app does
Screen readersEvery page has a heading that is announced when the page opens. Section titles are headings. Buttons and links carry their role and a name.
Text sizeText follows the phone's text size, tested up to 200%. One exception: the five bottom-tab labels stop growing at 130%, because "Accounts" and "Cash Flow" do not fit side by side above that. The tabs are still read out in full.
ColourText meets 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text). Field borders and icons meet 3:1. Status is never shown by colour alone: an overspent budget bar also has a hatch pattern.
MotionThe phone's reduce-motion setting turns off animations, in more than 50 places.
OrientationNot locked: portrait and landscape both work.
Time limitsThe app locks after a period without use. The user picks 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 minutes (default 2). Typing and screen-reader actions count as use, and the screen is kept as it was behind the lock. Sign-in codes last 10 minutes and can be resent after 60 seconds.
ErrorsError messages are announced by the screen reader when they appear, and each form field shows its own error.
Help (SEBI §4)"Trouble seeing or using the app? Tap here" on the sign-in and details screens, and under Profile. It offers a call-back from our helpdesk, a phone number and an email address, and lets the user record a disability (optional). Complaints have an "Accessibility" category.

1

Sign in

What it is for: Enter a mobile number, then the 6-digit code sent by SMS. How to reach it: First screen after install. Screens: 3

Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • The page title is read out when the screen opens.
  • The phone field is named; the "+91" prefix is not a separate stop.
  • A wrong or expired code, an invalid number or a failed send is read out as soon as it appears.
  • "Terms of Use" and "Privacy Policy" are links with 48 dp touch areas.
  • The code is valid for 10 minutes and can be resent after 60 seconds.
  • "Trouble seeing or using the app? Tap here" opens Call and Email options for our helpdesk (no account exists yet, so nothing is saved here).

Please check in particular

  • Reading and entering the 6-digit code with VoiceOver and TalkBack, including SMS auto-fill.
  • Focus order from number to code to the help link.
2

App lock

What it is for: Set a 4-digit PIN, unlock the app, and change lock settings. How to reach it: After first sign-in; the lock appears after the chosen idle time. Settings: Profile → App lock. Screens: 4

Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • Every PIN key is named and at least 48 dp.
  • A wrong PIN is read out.
  • Face ID or fingerprint can be used instead of the PIN.
  • "Lock after" is a choice of 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 minutes. Typing and screen-reader actions count as use.
  • Whatever the user was doing is kept behind the lock.

Please check in particular

  • How PIN progress (dots filled) is announced.
  • Moving between the system biometric prompt and the PIN pad with a screen reader.
3

Your details and accessibility help

What it is for: Enter PAN or date of birth so we can find the user's accounts. This is also where SEBI §4 help is offered. How to reach it: Right after sign-in, on first use. Screens: 6

Scroll sideways for more. Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • Date-of-birth cells are named Day, Month and Year.
  • PAN and date errors are read out when they appear.
  • "Trouble seeing or using the app? Tap here" opens the Accessibility help sheet: an optional "Do you have a disability?" choice (Seeing, Hearing, Movement, Other, No, Prefer not to say), a "Call me to help me sign up" switch, and Call and Email buttons.
  • Saving is confirmed out loud. A call-back request alerts our helpdesk.
  • If the date of birth shows the user is under 18, sign-up stops, with "Think this is wrong? Contact us" leading to a person.
  • The same help sheet is under Profile → Privacy & data, to change the answer later.

Please check in particular

  • Entering the PAN in its segmented field with a screen reader.
  • Whether the selected disability choice and the switch state are announced clearly.
4

Linking bank and investment accounts

What it is for: Choose banks and investment accounts, then approve sharing through an Account Aggregator. How to reach it: During first use, and from Accounts → Linked accounts. Screens: 6

Scroll sideways for more. Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • Bank tiles announce whether they are selected.
  • The ring showing what is linked is named, for example "0 of 4 kinds added".
  • The guide line at the top is read out when it changes.
  • The whole search box (not just the text line) can be tapped to type.
  • "Skip investments" and other links have 48 dp touch areas.
  • If the Account Aggregator cannot be reached in 20 seconds, the app says so and offers "Try again".

Please check in particular

  • Moving from the app to the Account Aggregator's page and back with a screen reader. (The Account Aggregator's page itself is run by them; see "Outside our app".)
  • The waiting state while the connection is made.

Cash flow

What it is for: Money in and out this month, budget by category, and where the money went. How to reach it: Cash Flow tab. Screens: 1

Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • Every amount is read with its sign, for example "Spent, minus 72 thousand rupees".
  • Each budget bar is read with its limit and status, for example "Obligations, 28 thousand rupees, of ₹40K limit, On track".
  • An overspent bar has a hatch pattern, not only a colour.
  • Section titles are headings.
  • At 200% text the month tabs scroll sideways instead of being cut off.

Please check in particular

  • Whether the month switcher and the bars make sense when heard in order.

Finwy (questions and answers)

What it is for: Ask money questions in plain words; answers can include short charts. How to reach it: Finwy tab (the first tab). Screens: 2

Select a screenshot to open it full size.

Already in place

  • A finished answer is read out.
  • A failed or limited answer is also read out.
  • Charts are read one month at a time, for example "Apr, ₹51.8K".

  • Typing a question with the phone's voice dictation.
